# Playbook

The rules. Written down so decisions are made before the market opens, not during it.

## 1. Money split ($25,000)

| Bucket | Amount | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| **Long-term core** | $20,000 (80%) | Quality stocks + QQQ. No panic selling, no trading. Review monthly. Over time diversify beyond AI (e.g. part in VOO/SPY). No single stock above 10% of the total |
| **Trading bucket** | $5,000 (20%) | Only the strategies below |
| ↳ options | max $1,000 at risk per week | Max $250–500 on one options trade |

Core add-on rule: keep ~10% of the core in cash and buy in pieces when QQQ is −10%, −20%, −30% from its high.

## 2. Risk limits (trading bucket)

- Max loss per trade: **$250** (1% of $25k). Know the exit before buying.
- Daily loss limit: **−$250** → done for the day.
- Weekly loss limit: **−$500** → done for the week.
- 2 losing trades in a row → no new trade that day.
- Trading bucket down 30% (to $3,500) → paper only until the strategy proves itself again.
- Profit above $5,000 moves to the core each month.
- Same-day options: out by **3:15 PM ET** (Robinhood may auto-sell expiring in-the-money options from ~3:30 PM).

## 3. Strategies

### A. Deep-dip quality (strongest evidence)
- **Setup:** a quality stock is ≥30% below its 52-week high **while QQQ is ≥10% below its high** (market-driven fall), and the company itself has no bad news (earnings fine).
- **Buy** in 2–3 pieces. Hold 6–12 months. Max 10% of the portfolio per stock.
- Avoid stocks that fell on their own (QQQ near its high): those recovered much more slowly.

### B. Down-day basket
- **Trigger:** QQQ down ≥1% on the day (paper experiment buys every day for learning).
- **Buy 7 stocks together** (biggest fallers near their day low from the pool, skipping any with company-specific bad news).
- **Sell all together** at the basket target. **30-day limit**: sell whatever the result.
- Works on a basket or on QQQ/SPY. **Not on single stocks**: one stuck stock (PYPL, ZM, SMCI) can wipe out years of small wins.

### C. Earnings bets (test on paper first)
- Prefer **shares** or buying **after** a strong report (post-earnings drift) over options before the report.
- If using options: 2+ weeks to expiration, and only when the implied move is **smaller** than the stock's usual earnings move.
- Log every bet. Judge after ~20 bets.

### D. Intraday QQQ dip (paper only)
- QQQ ≥1% below its open before noon ET, then a 5-minute bar closes above the prior two bars' highs.
- Backtest edge was thin (~+3.6% over a year, 41 trades). Not for real money until proven.

## 4. Habits

- No stop-loss orders on long-term shares; use price alerts and review instead.
- Before any trade: setup present? size within limits? earnings or Fed today? exit plan?
- Weekly: compare results with simply holding QQQ, and count rules broken.
